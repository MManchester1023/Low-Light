# Low Light: changelog

| Version | Date | Changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| v0.1 | 2026-11-26 | Weekend 1 prototype: Three.js room with crates and a divider wall, third-person player with crouch and run, switchable light groups and desk lamp, light meter, patrolling guard with view cone, suspicion meter and investigate/look states, objective, spotted/complete screens, R restart, V shows guard view cone. Local server via `node serve.js`. |
| v0.1.1 | 2026-11-26 | Fix: "Click to play" did nothing when the browser refused mouse lock. Clicking now always starts play; without a lock you look by dragging the mouse or with the arrow keys. Esc or P pauses. The page now explains if the game fails to load (opened as a file, or Three.js couldn't download). |
| v0.2 | 2026-11-26 | Player footstep noise (crouch 1 m / walk 4 m / run 10 m, halved through walls) with a NOISE meter; guards hear and investigate sounds (sound caps suspicion at 75). Guards notice dead lights and walk over to switch them back on. Guard nav graph so they path through doorways. Night vision toggle (N) as a full-screen shader. V now also shows the noise ring. |
| v0.2.1 | 2026-11-26 | Night vision much brighter in dark rooms: log amplification curve instead of a flat gain (the dark room rendered ~200x dimmer than lit areas, so the old gain barely lifted it). |
| v0.3 | 2026-11-26 | Guards turn and walk to footsteps they hear (once they have some suspicion), look around, then return to patrol warier; hearing again while looking around sends them to the new spot. First hand-to-hand takedown: press E within 1.4 m of an unaware guard's rear arc to grab him, drag him (2.5 m noise), press E again to knock him out (3 s struggle, 1.5 m noise); he slumps and stays down. Unbroken grab after 8 s counts as spotted. Takedown prompts take priority over switches. Capsule pose animation only. Rating: Shadow if a guard was touched, Ghost if not. |
| v0.3.1 | 2026-11-26 | Fix: bottom HUD overlap. The interact prompt and the light/noise meter now sit in one stacked column (14 px gap), so the prompt can no longer cover the meter. |
| v0.4 | 2026-11-26 | Throwable bottle in the test level (on the crate near the start): E pick up, hold G to aim with an arc preview, release to throw (10 m/s). It smashes on impact with an 8 m noise, halved through walls (guess); guards who hear it get +20 suspicion (capped at 75 like other sounds), turn, walk over and look around. V shows the smash noise ring. Guard view cone 90° → 120° per the updated design doc. Design doc renamed to `Design Doc.md`; CLAUDE.md and status updated (new roadmap items: guard memory, open-ended levels). |
| v0.5 | 2026-11-26 | Guard memory: noises he investigated, sightings and dead lights (remembered at the switch) become remembered spots for the mission; events within 3 m stack (sight counts double). New calm 'check' state: every 20 s of patrol (guess) he walks to the busiest, least recently checked spot, looks around 3.5 s and resumes the route; noise or sight during it interrupts as normal. V shows remembered spots as orange rings sized by weight. Restart clears memory. |
| docs | 2026-11-26 | Open questions answered: guard state flow and two-press takedown confirmed; body hiding moved into the prototype; full alert planned as guards sharing info and hunting the player. Roadmap renumbered. |
